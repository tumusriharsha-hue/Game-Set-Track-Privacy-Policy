GameSetTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. About This Policy

GameSetTrack is a tennis tracking and analytics application developed and managed by Sriharsha Tumu and published by Ravichandra Tumu through an individual Apple Developer account.

This Privacy Policy explains how GameSetTrack ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our iPhone app, Apple Watch app, and related services.

Ravichandra Tumu is the publisher responsible for the application's privacy practices. Sriharsha Tumu develops and maintains GameSetTrack.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, GameSetTrack may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, account identifiers, and authentication-related information.
  • Tennis information: Match records, scores, points, match dates, opponent names or labels, match notes, and other information you enter.
  • Performance information: Calculated statistics, trends, match summaries, and analytics based on tennis activity.
  • AI coaching information: Coaching requests, relevant match statistics, performance summaries, generated recommendations, and related analysis.
  • Synchronization information: Record identifiers, update information, synchronization status, and data used to resolve conflicts across devices.
  • Technical information: Information necessary for authentication, request validation, rate limiting, troubleshooting, and service security.

GameSetTrack does not require medical records, HealthKit access, or health information to provide its tennis tracking features.

3. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In

GameSetTrack supports Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In for authentication.

When you sign in, your selected identity provider supplies authentication information that Supabase Auth uses to establish and maintain your GameSetTrack account.

Depending on the provider and information you choose to share, this may include an email address, name, and provider-specific account identifier.

Sign in with Apple may allow you to use Apple's Hide My Email feature, which provides a private relay email address.

GameSetTrack does not receive your Apple Account or Google account password.

Supabase Auth manages application sessions, user identifiers, and authentication tokens. The iPhone manages sign-in and communicates relevant authentication state to the companion Apple Watch app.

Provider privacy information:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

4. Local Storage

GameSetTrack uses Apple's SwiftData framework to store tennis records and application data locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Local information may include match history, point records, statistics, notes, and information awaiting synchronization.

This allows supported features to work without an active internet connection.

When cloud synchronization is enabled and connectivity is available, eligible locally stored information may be uploaded to the backend.

Local information may remain on a device until removed through app functionality, account deletion, or applicable device storage behavior.

5. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

GameSetTrack uses Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL database storage, and server-side Edge Functions.

Match records, point-level information, and related tennis data may be synchronized to Supabase through authenticated requests.

Synchronization uses account identifiers and access tokens to associate records with the appropriate user.

The backend implements ownership validation, conflict handling, offline queue processing, and retry mechanisms.

Cloud synchronization helps restore and maintain tennis records across supported devices signed into the same account.

Supabase processes information according to its applicable contractual terms and privacy practices.

Learn more: Supabase Privacy Policy .

6. Apple Watch and WatchConnectivity

GameSetTrack includes an Apple Watch companion application for recording tennis matches and individual points.

The Apple Watch and iPhone applications communicate through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework.

This communication may include match records, point updates, synchronization information, and relevant authentication state.

The iPhone serves as the primary coordinator for cloud synchronization.

GameSetTrack does not use WatchConnectivity for advertising or cross-app tracking.

7. AI Coaching and NVIDIA NIM

GameSetTrack offers AI-assisted tennis coaching and performance analysis.

When you request AI coaching, relevant match statistics, performance information, and coaching context may be transmitted through authenticated Supabase Edge Functions to NVIDIA NIM.

NVIDIA's AI services process the submitted information to generate coaching recommendations and structured responses.

GameSetTrack does not intentionally send Apple or Google passwords, authentication tokens, or privileged backend credentials to the AI model.

Coaching responses and related analysis may be cached locally or in backend storage for performance and reuse.

NVIDIA may process or retain request information according to its applicable agreements, service configuration, and privacy policies.

Learn more: NVIDIA Privacy Center .

AI-generated coaching recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are provided for general tennis education and improvement, not as medical or professional health advice.

8. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and restore sessions.
  • Record matches, scores, and points.
  • Calculate tennis statistics and trends.
  • Synchronize data between supported devices.
  • Support offline tracking and queued synchronization.
  • Generate AI-assisted coaching recommendations.
  • Cache generated analysis when appropriate.
  • Resolve synchronization conflicts.
  • Prevent unauthorized access and service abuse.
  • Maintain application reliability and security.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

9. Third-Party Services

GameSetTrack uses the following services to provide its functionality:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, SwiftData, WatchConnectivity, and device services.
  • Google: Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Supabase: Authentication, PostgreSQL storage, cloud synchronization, Edge Functions, and backend security.
  • NVIDIA: AI inference for tennis coaching features.

These providers may process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

10. Advertising and Tracking

GameSetTrack does not currently use advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party cross-app tracking features.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or use tennis performance data for targeted advertising.

Third-party authentication and infrastructure providers may process technical information according to their own privacy practices.

11. Data Sharing

Information is transmitted to third-party service providers only as needed to operate the supported features described in this policy.

Supabase may process account details and synchronized tennis records. NVIDIA may process relevant performance information submitted for AI coaching.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or legitimate security needs.

GameSetTrack does not intentionally make private user account information publicly available to other users.

12. Data Retention

Account information and cloud-synchronized tennis records may be retained while your account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

Local information may remain on your iPhone or Apple Watch until deleted through the application or applicable device storage processes.

Cached coaching responses, technical logs, and synchronization information may be retained for functionality, reliability, and security.

Independent service providers may retain certain request records and other processed information under their own policies and agreements.

We aim to limit unnecessary retention, subject to applicable legal obligations.

13. Security

GameSetTrack uses HTTPS, Supabase authentication, access tokens, and backend ownership validation to protect account-related operations.

Privileged Supabase credentials and NVIDIA API credentials are managed server-side and are not included in the production apps.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

No electronic transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Account Deletion

GameSetTrack provides an in-app account deletion process.

Account deletion requests are processed using a Supabase Edge Function that handles associated account data cleanup.

Following successful deletion, GameSetTrack clears applicable local account information, signs the user out, and removes account-associated data covered by the server-side deletion process.

Certain records may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security obligations.

Independent providers may retain information under their own applicable retention practices.

For deletion assistance or privacy requests, email gamesettrackapp@gmail.com .

15. Children's Privacy

GameSetTrack is intended for a general tennis-playing audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without authorization required by applicable law.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, they may contact us to request review or deletion.

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.
